About

Storytelling pitch for a new thriller IP

This second-year solo university project was created in less than three weeks. The time constraints and complexity made the idea risky, but the project went forward and the audience was fully engaged, frightened, and anxious.

Premise

Samira's investigation

Pitched as a new IP reminiscent of action psychological horror games like Resident Evil 7 and F.E.A.R., the story follows young detective Samira searching for her partner and father figure Joe after he disappears while investigating a disturbing number of missing people.

Her own life is put in danger as the connections become terrifying and chilling, pointing to a dreadful conspiracy. The concept mixes action sequences with slow-burning investigation and puzzle solving.

Aspects worked on

Cinematics, tech, design, research

Cinematic features

  • Mixed Legacy Matinee and Level Sequencers with sound and animations for impactful scenes.
  • Replaced certain assets, such as a burning car from an undamaged version, to imply damage.
  • Used animated cameras and slow motion during key sequences to heighten suspense.
  • Applied lighting to create a consistent mood.
  • Mixed a song so the title arrival became more memorable.

Tech features

  • Responsive UI floating over interactable items.
  • Interactable doors, drawers, and cupboards.
  • Mouse-rotated item examination.
  • Dynamic narrative comment tool for items the player interacts with.
  • 3D audio and Blueprint scripting for rapid prototyping.

Design features

  • Designed posters to sell the idea.
  • Created original and intuitive UI icons.
  • Modelled placeholder items to help convey the storyline.
  • Used decals to decorate key items.

Research and development

  • Setting inspired by Taylor Sheridan's Wind River.
  • Studied pacing from Mindhunter and Hannibal for event timing.
  • Looked into Silent Hill to learn more about unnerving audio atmosphere.
