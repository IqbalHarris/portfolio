Support options

Support can be light-touch or hands-on, from quick directional feedback through to deeper combat tuning and design input.

Build or video feedback

Design-focused notes on combat feel, readability, pacing, and the tuning priorities that deserve attention first.

Melee feel and timing

Attack commitment, responsiveness, hit reactions, parry flow, execution feel, and the spaces where combat loses trust.

Loop and system direction

Documentation and notes

Clear written breakdowns, priority recommendations, and next steps your team can act on.
