Support options

How I can help.

Support can be light-touch or hands-on, from quick directional feedback through to deeper work on systems, game feel, core loops, and design direction.

Build or video feedback

I can test your build or provide feedback on detailed gameplay video, highlighting the priorities that deserve attention first.

Addictive combat feel

Whether your combat is melee, ranged, ability-led, or hybrid, strong feedback, meaningful strategy, responsiveness, and clear reactions help make it hard to put down.

Strong core loops and game direction

I can help shape how systems, resources, and rewards reinforce your core loop and support the wider direction of the game.

Documentation and notes

Clear written breakdowns, priority recommendations, and next steps your team can act on.
